# Lowballer style, for Countless Days

The person asked for the landing page "in the lowballer style" (sgilson7/lowballer): the warm street of Lowballer's title picture, which itself follows Sam_Comm_final.png in the vagrancy repository.

- A mustard sky in flat bands, puffy clouds outlined as one shape (`motifs.cloud`).
- Salmon, coral, pink and sage buildings with lit and dark windows, antennas and rooftop clutter.
- Bunting strung between buildings in plum, gold, teal, coral and pink.
- One oversized subject on the street: here a boombox, with the game's targets and approach rings floating above it like balloons, and cassettes and records at its feet.
- Flat fills only; every shape outlined in the one plum `ink`, round joins and caps.
- No letters, words or numbers in any picture: the page sets every word.
- The upper left stays calm (sky and clouds) for the title.
